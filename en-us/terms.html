Terms of Use

Effective June 19, 2026

Follower Mirror is a personal tool for analyzing files that you export from your own account and select in the app. The raw file is read from the location you choose and is not retained after it has been read.

The app is not affiliated with Meta or Instagram and is not sponsored, endorsed, or approved by either company. You are responsible for obtaining exports through the tools provided by the platform where you hold the account and for following that platform's terms.

App limits

The app does not ask for credentials or passwords, sign in to accounts, use tokens or cookies, access private APIs, scrape data, or automate account actions.

Follower Mirror does not provide or promise real-time monitoring or tracking. Differences appear only after you import a newer export. The app compares selected files; it does not determine why a name appears or is missing.

Storage and Pro

Processed records remain on your device by default. Pro users may enable private iCloud sync, which stores only processed data in the user's private CloudKit database. The developer does not operate a follower-data server.

Pro is offered as an auto-renewable annual subscription managed by Apple or a lifetime non-consumable purchase managed by Apple. Pro may unlock complete lists, your second and subsequent imports, unlimited local history, comparisons, advanced metrics, the Pro report, private iCloud sync, reminders, CSV export, and a local HTML report.

Apple manages purchases, annual renewals, cancellations, and charges through the user's Apple Account, including the process for restoring purchases. Refund requests follow Apple's rules. The lifetime purchase does not renew automatically.

Results and deletion

Results depend on the completeness, date, and format of the files you select. The app is a personal organization and analysis tool; it does not guarantee the current state of an account on another platform.

You can delete individual imports, erase all local data, turn off iCloud sync, and, if you have Pro, delete processed data synced to iCloud from within the app under Settings > Privacy and Data.
