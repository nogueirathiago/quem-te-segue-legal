Support

Help importing and comparing your exports.

The app does not access your account. To see changes, import a newer export that you downloaded.

Import your data

Download a connections export outside the app, choose JSON when available, then select the ZIP, JSON, or HTML file from Files on your iPhone.

Fix a file error

Check that the export includes followers and following. If the ZIP file won't open or content is missing, download a new export.

Compare changes

Differences appear after you import a newer export. The app does not monitor your account in real time or infer why a relationship changed.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need my password or account access?

No. It does not ask for credentials, sign in, collect cookies, use tokens, access private APIs, scrape data, or automate account actions.

Where is my data stored?

Processed imports stay on your device by default. Pro users may enable private iCloud sync for processed data. The raw file you select is not retained after it has been read.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings > Privacy and Data to erase local data, turn off iCloud sync, or request deletion of processed Pro data synced to iCloud. You can also delete individual imports.

How are Pro purchases handled?

Apple manages the auto-renewable annual subscription and lifetime non-consumable purchase through your Apple Account, including the process for restoring purchases.

How can I contact support?

Email thenrique.nogueira@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue.

Do not send your password. Never send passwords, tokens, cookies, authentication codes, or files containing data you do not want to share.
