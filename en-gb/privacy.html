Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 June 2026

Quem te segue is a personal tool for analysing account export files that you select. It is not affiliated with Meta or Instagram and is not sponsored, endorsed or approved by either company.

The app does not request Instagram credentials or passwords, sign in to accounts, collect cookies, use access tokens, access private APIs, scrape data or automate account actions.

Information processed by the app

When you select a connections ZIP, JSON or HTML file, the app processes usernames from the followers and following lists locally. Processed records may contain normalised usernames, counts, an import date, an import label, a source hash, local review preferences and comparisons between imports.

The raw file is read from the location you select and is not retained once it has been read.

Storage and device authentication

Processed records stay on your device by default. Pro users may switch on private iCloud sync. When enabled, only processed data is stored in the user's private CloudKit database and counts towards that user's iCloud storage. The developer does not run a follower-data server.

The optional app lock relies on the device's authentication. Quem te segue does not collect, receive, transmit or store Face ID, Touch ID or passcode data.

Tracking and accuracy

This version contains no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising. It does not sell user data or share processed records with Meta or Instagram.

The app neither monitors accounts in real time nor retrieves profile data within the app. It compares selected files and does not infer why a relationship changed, so results may not reflect the current state of an account.

Deletion and purchases

You can delete individual imports, erase all local data, switch off iCloud sync and, if you have Pro, delete processed data synced to iCloud from within the app under Settings > Privacy and Data.

Pro is available as an auto-renewable annual subscription or a lifetime non-consumable purchase. Apple manages purchases, renewals, cancellations, charges, refund requests and restoration through the user's Apple Account. The lifetime purchase does not renew automatically.
