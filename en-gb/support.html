Support

Help with importing and comparing your exports.

The app cannot access your account directly. Import a newer export that you downloaded whenever you want to compare changes.

Import an export

Download your connections data outside the app, select JSON where available, then choose the ZIP, JSON or HTML file through Files on your iPhone.

Resolve a file problem

Make sure the export contains followers and following. If the ZIP file cannot be opened or content is missing, download another export.

Check for differences

The app shows differences after a newer export is imported. It does not monitor accounts live or infer the reason for a change.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need my password or account access?

No. It does not ask for credentials, sign in, collect cookies, use tokens, access private APIs, scrape data or automate account actions.

Where are processed records kept?

Processed imports remain on your device by default. Pro users may switch on private iCloud sync for processed data. The raw file you choose is not retained once it has been read.

How can I erase my data?

Open Settings > Privacy and Data to remove local data, switch off iCloud sync or request deletion of processed Pro data synced to iCloud. Individual imports can also be deleted.

Who manages Pro purchases?

Apple manages the auto-renewable annual subscription and lifetime non-consumable purchase through your Apple Account, including the process for restoring purchases.

How do I contact support?

Email thenrique.nogueira@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a description of the problem.

Never send your password. Do not share passwords, tokens, cookies, authentication codes or files containing data you do not wish to share.
